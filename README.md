> **Your AI-powered culinary companion for discovering, planning, cooking, understanding, and enjoying better meals.**

Savorly is a modern **AI-powered food discovery, meal planning, nutrition, grocery, pantry, and cooking intelligence platform** designed to transform everyday cooking into a personalized experience.

Instead of simply displaying recipes, Savorly understands the user's:

* Food preferences
* Dietary requirements
* Allergies
* Available ingredients
* Pantry inventory
* Cooking skill
* Available time
* Nutrition goals
* Budget
* Meal schedule
* Cooking history
* Favorite cuisines

and uses this information to generate personalized recommendations and actions.

# ⚖️ Responsible Food AI

Savorly's nutrition and allergy information should be treated as informational support, not medical diagnosis or individualized medical treatment.

The platform should:

* Clearly display nutrition data sources where applicable
* Flag uncertain nutrition estimates
* Avoid claiming medical outcomes
* Treat allergies as safety-critical information
* Encourage users to verify packaged-food labels
* Provide clear warnings when ingredient information is incomplete
