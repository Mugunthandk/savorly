# 🍽️ SAVORLY

## AI-Powered Culinary Intelligence & Personal Food Companion

> **Discover → Understand → Plan → Shop → Cook → Track → Learn → Recommend**

Savorly is an AI-powered culinary intelligence platform designed to transform the way people discover recipes, plan meals, manage groceries, understand nutrition, cook food, reduce waste, and build personalized eating habits.

Instead of functioning as a traditional recipe website, Savorly acts as a **personal food intelligence companion** that continuously learns from a user's preferences, dietary requirements, available ingredients, cooking behavior, nutrition goals, budget, pantry inventory, and meal history.

---
